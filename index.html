How we pave

Four steps to a solid driveway

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Site visit & measure

    We look at the area, take measurements and agree on the layout, levels and finish with you.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Excavate & prepare

    The old surface comes out, the ground is levelled and the base is compacted so nothing sinks later.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    Lay & compact

    Pavers are laid to pattern and cut to fit, or tar is spread and rolled, then it’s all compacted with a plate compactor.

  4. 4

    Step 4

    Finish & clean up

    Edges are set, joints are sanded and the site is swept clean, ready to drive or walk on.
